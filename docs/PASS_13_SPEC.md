# twoballoons Pass 13 Specification: Cinematic Presentation Mode, Keyframing & Interactive Storyboards

## 1. Overview
Pass 13 transforms twoballoons into a presentation powerhouse, allowing architects to pitch, present, and walk stakeholders through architectures with zero external slide tools.

---

## 2. Core Capabilities
1. **Interactive Keyframe Frames**: Define slides as viewport regions on the infinite canvas.
2. **Cinematic Camera Interpolation**: Smooth pan, zoom, and focus easing between macro-systems and micro-components.
3. **Presenter Spotlight & Laser HUD**: Ephemeral trails, focus dimming, and interactive slide mode.
